Schwamm Drauf!

Schwamm Drauf! is a data story about the climate in the city of Bamberg. It shows the importance of fine-grained measurement in inner cities (with different climate zones) and the varying temperatures in dependence of urban structures. As one solution to overheated city centers, the story introduces the concept of a sponge city.

baRADies?! is a data story about cycling in and around Bamberg. Using data from the campaign "Stadtradeln" in 2024 the story offers various perspectives on how cyclists use the traffic infrastructure. The users can personalize the story by taking a short survey at the beginning, and the responses are then used to customize the narrative and the interactive elements (choropleth and heat maps as well as bar charts, chord and arc diagrams).
